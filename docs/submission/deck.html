The borrower problemCureBid

Repayment without selling collateral.

A borrower with liquidity on Creditcoin can request repayment of an existing Aave USDC debt on Sepolia. Providers quote a total CTC reimbursement for a fixed repayment target. No invented dollar conversion; the borrower chooses the quote.

One source protocol. One debt asset. Two competing providers.
How it worksCureBid

A debt obligation, not a transfer.

Fund a capped request on Creditcoin → choose a provider → the provider repays Aave through CureRouter → Attestcoin authenticates the receipt → escrow credits the selected provider. Unused budget becomes borrower credit.

Source debt reduction and destination settlement are separate confirmations.
Attestcoin integrationCureBid

Proof is the payment authority.

The native Creditcoin verifier authenticates a source receipt. CureBid then requires successful execution, the approved router outcome and matching Aave repayment log, exact parties, amount, deadline and request domain. Relayers cannot redirect reimbursement.

Proof-safe expiryCureBid

Late proof must not steal a payment.

CureRouter records either Repaid or Expired for the full terms digest. Assigned escrow can pay from a proven repayment or refund from a proven expiry. A timer alone cannot refund away an honest provider’s reimbursement.

Trade-off: attestation outages can lock assigned escrow. No administrator override.
